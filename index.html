Guiding Philosophy

Restoring ancient equilibrium.

Our interventions are quiet, biologically disciplined, and focused on self-sustaining ecological regeneration.

01

Microclimate Stability

We nurture native high-canopy trees that regulate ambient soil humidity and protect fragile lichen and fungal mycelium colonies.

02

Riparian Filtration

Natural stone terraces and reed basins purify alpine snowmelt into pristine drinking streams without mechanical or chemical filtration.

03

Quiet Architecture

Low-impact cedar observation shelters built exclusively on pin foundations, completely reversible to leave no scar on the forest floor.

Old-Growth Canopy Mist

Rising over 65 meters above the valley floor, the upper canopy is a sheltered realm of ancient Sitka spruce and Douglas fir. Moisture from coastal fog condensates here, feeding rare hanging epiphyte gardens.

  • Ancient Sitka Spruce & Western Hemlock
  • Nesting corridors for rare marbled murrelets
